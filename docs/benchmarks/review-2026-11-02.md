# Benchmark review — 2026-11-02

[Calibration run](https://github.com/dornol/excel-kit/actions/runs/41411541574) ran five fresh JVM samples at commit `a501b6114c8fb0397816056680a09cbd83e9df76` on one AMD EPYC 9V45 96-Core Processor runner. All 17 scenarios used JDK 21.0.13, a 1 GiB heap and a 10 ms sampling interval. The profile records environment metadata, sample checksums and evidence URL.

Earlier main runs 41382234494 and 41404569272 used Intel Xeon 8573C, while 41386678917 used AMD EPYC 7763. They span different library revisions, so their differences cannot establish measurement noise. Hardware variation also means this single calibration profile will often be incompatible with future GitHub-hosted runners. Such runs retain measurements and compatible-baseline deltas without calibrated warnings.

These five samples are a pilot, not a statistical confidence bound or release performance budget. Cold starts, GC carryover and 10 ms sampling affect results. In particular, near-zero temporary peaks may be missed by sampling. Collect more repeated samples before tightening thresholds.
